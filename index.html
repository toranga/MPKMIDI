MPK Mini Player

Plug in your MPK mini, then click Start. The browser will ask for permission to use MIDI devices — click Allow.

Works in Chrome or Edge (Firefox needs an add-on, Safari doesn't support Web MIDI).

Pads (drums)

Pads silent? Turn off the CC button on the MPK, or press Learn pads, click a drum here, then hit the pad on your MPK.

Knobs

Keys

Use the Oct −/+ buttons on the MPK to shift range. Joystick X = pitch bend, joystick Y / mod = vibrato. Sustain pedal works too. You can also click the pads and keys with the mouse.

Sequencer · 1 bar of 4/4 (16 steps)

Click or drag to draw. Space = play/stop.
